Questions

Asked by firms like yours

What does ConveneMD charge?

A flat 20% service fee on the expert's billed fees, itemized on every invoice — charged only when you retain an expert. Searching, dossiers, and screening calls are free. Founding Firms (our first 10) lock the 20% rate permanently; the standard rate will be 30%.

How does payment work?

One vendor, one bill. Your firm funds a retainer with ConveneMD against a written estimate; the expert invoices us, we audit the time against the estimate, and we pay the expert 100% of their rate. Unused retainer is refunded at case close. You never receive invoices from individual physicians.

Can you guarantee the expert supports our case?

No — and any service that does should worry you. Our experts are independent physicians who give their honest opinion; that independence is what makes their testimony credible. What we guarantee is speed, vetting, transparency, and process.

How are experts vetted?

Board certification, active licensure in your case state, active clinical practice, disciplinary history, prior testimony record, and a conflicts check against the named parties — verified before a dossier ever reaches you.

What if you can't fill my case?

You will know within 4 business hours, with a straight explanation. No stringing along, and of course no charge.

What happens if my expert has to withdraw mid-case?

We source and onboard a replacement at no charge, and our service fee is waived until the replacement is current on the record.

Who runs ConveneMD?

A practicing physician with 20 years in medicine who has personally served as a retained expert — ConveneMD was built from the expert's side of the table, which is why firms and physicians both get a fair structure.
