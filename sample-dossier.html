How every ConveneMD dossier is built

  1. Verified before you see it — license, board certification, active-practice status, and disciplinary history checked against primary sources.
  2. Conflicts-cleared — candidates are checked against your named parties and receive no case details until the check clears.
  3. Testimony on the table — full history with plaintiff/defense split and any exclusions, so nothing surprises you on cross.
  4. True rates — the expert sets their rate and keeps 100% of it. ConveneMD's flat, itemized 20% service fee is added on top, only if you retain, as its own line on every invoice.
  5. Free screening calls — speak with finalists before you decide. If we ever miss the 24-hour window on a common specialty, our fee on that case is $0.
