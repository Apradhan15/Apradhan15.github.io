How every ConveneMD dossier is built

  1. 01

    Verified before you see it

    License, board certification, active-practice status, and disciplinary history checked against primary sources.

  2. 02

    Conflicts-cleared

    Candidates are checked against your named parties and receive no case details until the check clears.

  3. 03

    Testimony on the table

    Full history with plaintiff/defense split and any exclusions, so nothing surprises you on cross.

    True rates

    The expert sets their rate and keeps 100% of it. ConveneMD's flat, itemized 20% service fee is added on top, only if you retain, as its own line on every invoice.
